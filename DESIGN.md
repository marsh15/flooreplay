# FloorReplay design

FloorReplay is a quiet evidence workbench. Its visual identity follows the manufacturing investigation: recorded output, source rows, knowledge cutoffs, immutable revisions, and human decisions. It uses warm neutral page surfaces, white documents, charcoal navigation, and restrained amber for evidence limitations and observed shortfall. Status text always explains color.

Inter is the interface face. JetBrains Mono is self-hosted for source identifiers, formulas, and structured records. Headings use a clear size hierarchy with modest negative tracking. Numeric tables use tabular figures. No decorative charts, confidence gauges, neon accents, or staged entrances.

The shell shares one 72rem content grid. Mobile navigation wraps into its own row. Pages have a title, explanation, task controls, and report content in that order. Incident investigations offer direct section links. Related production measurements form one divided document row rather than disconnected dashboard cards.

Controls provide visible focus and short feedback. Frequent inspection actions stay immediate. Popovers dismiss with Escape, restore trigger focus, and align to their trigger. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Touch controls have larger hit areas. Tables scroll within their containers; incident library records have a narrow-screen reading layout.

Saved deterministic bundles, live analyses, historical model outputs, and unavailable capabilities retain explicit labels. Design changes must preserve evidence provenance and synthetic-data notices.
